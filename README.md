<h1 class="heading-element" dir="auto" tabindex="-1">About Us:</h1>
<p>Downtown Pain Physicians Of Brooklyn is a <a href="https://www.google.com/maps?cid=4485960528087640565">pain management clinic in Brooklyn, NY</a>, located at 145 Henry Street, #1G near Brooklyn Heights and Downtown Brooklyn. Led by Dr. Raj Raval, M.D., the practice provides highly personalized and comprehensive pain care from experienced <a href="https://www.downtownpainphysicians.com/pain-management-doctors-specialists/">pain management doctors</a> and top-rated New York pain specialists.</p>
<p>Dr. Raval is a Fellowship-trained Interventional Pain &amp; Musculoskeletal Medicine Specialist with advanced training in Physical Medicine &amp; Rehabilitation, interventional pain management, and musculoskeletal medicine. The clinic focuses on identifying the source of pain before recommending treatment, using a careful evaluation process that may include physical examination, imaging review, functional assessment, medication management when appropriate, rehabilitation guidance, and interventional treatment planning.</p>
<p>Downtown Pain Physicians Of Brooklyn uses modern pain management technology, state-of-the-art equipment, and advanced image-guided techniques to treat spine, joint, nerve, and musculoskeletal pain. The practice provides non-surgical and minimally invasive treatment options for <a href="https://www.downtownpainphysicians.com/back-pain-management/">back pain</a>, neck pain, sciatica, herniated disc pain, knee pain, shoulder pain, hip pain, sports injuries, arthritis pain, nerve pain, acute pain, and chronic pain conditions.</p>
<p>Treatment options may include <a href="https://www.downtownpainphysicians.com/epidural-steroid-injections/">epidural steroid injections</a>, facet joint injections, sacroiliac joint injections, medial branch blocks, trigger point injections, nerve blocks, joint injections, cortisone injections, knee injection therapy, viscosupplementation, lumbar radiofrequency ablation, cervical radiofrequency ablation, spinal cord stimulation, prolotherapy, regenerative therapy, kyphoplasty, vertebroplasty, endoscopic discectomy, and other fluoroscopic or ultrasound-guided minimally invasive procedures when clinically appropriate.</p>
<p>Care is centered on reducing pain, improving mobility, supporting daily function, and helping patients return to normal activity whenever possible. Downtown Pain Physicians Of Brooklyn serves patients from Brooklyn Heights, Downtown Brooklyn, DUMBO, Cobble Hill, Boerum Hill, Vinegar Hill, Williamsburg, Fort Greene, Carroll Gardens, and nearby New York City communities.</p>
<p>Downtown Pain Physicians Of Brooklyn is located at 145 Henry Street, #1G, Brooklyn, NY 11201. To schedule an appointment, call 718-521-2424 or visit <a href="https://www.downtownpainphysicians.com/">https://www.downtownpainphysicians.com/</a></p>
